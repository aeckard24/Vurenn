# Vurenn product roadmap

This is a product backlog, not a promise that unavailable integrations already work. Each item should ship only after privacy, cost, abuse, accessibility, and load testing.

## Truth and decision tools

1. **Claim ledger** — split an answer into verifiable claims with source and confidence.
2. **Contradiction radar** — surface conflicts across uploaded files, chats, and sources.
3. **Evidence freshness monitor** — warn when a relied-on source has become stale.
4. **Assumption switchboard** — let users toggle assumptions and regenerate the conclusion.
5. **Steelman view** — build the strongest credible case against the current answer.
6. **Decision journal** — save the evidence, decision, owner, and later outcome.
7. **Outcome follow-up** — ask whether advice worked and improve future recommendations.
8. **Uncertainty controls** — choose conservative, balanced, or exploratory conclusions.
9. **Verification budget** — decide how much time and money a result deserves.
10. **Truth report** — export sources, claims, uncertainty, dissent, and limitations together.

## Personal intelligence

11. **Memory receipts** — show exactly which saved fact influenced an answer.
12. **Privacy zones** — mark memories as session-only, project-only, or account-wide.
13. **Personal glossary** — learn a user's acronyms, products, people, and preferred terms.
14. **Answer versioning** — compare revisions and explain why the answer changed.
15. **Stakeholder lenses** — view a plan as a customer, engineer, finance lead, or critic.
16. **Constraint cards** — permanently attach budget, deadline, risk, and policy limits.
17. **Reusable project briefs** — turn project context into a living one-page brief.
18. **Next-action contracts** — convert advice into owners, dates, and measurable outcomes.
19. **Conversation bookmarks** — save a precise voice or chat moment with its context.
20. **Offline draft mode** — write and organize private drafts before sending anything.

## Research and files

21. **Research replay** — inspect every query, page, exclusion, and synthesis step.
22. **Why this tool** — disclose why Vurenn chose web, files, code, or image generation.
23. **Source inbox** — forward links and files into a project for later verification.
24. **File relationship graph** — reveal references, contradictions, and dependencies.
25. **Spreadsheet formula audit** — flag suspicious formulas, broken references, and outliers.
26. **Document red team** — find weak claims and likely objections before publication.
27. **Browser evidence capture** — save a dated, cited snapshot of important evidence.
28. **Research change alerts** — notify users when new evidence changes a saved conclusion.
29. **Data provenance trail** — show the origin and transformations behind every result.
30. **Review checkpoints** — pause high-impact research for scoped human approval.

## Voice and visual work

31. **Interruption-aware voice** — stop speaking immediately and retain the interrupted point.
32. **Voice action confirmation** — require explicit approval before consequential actions.
33. **Live conversation recap** — maintain a small, editable list of agreed facts and tasks.
34. **Personal pronunciation** — privately learn names and domain-specific pronunciations.
35. **Accessibility voice profiles** — optimize pace, pauses, captions, and cognitive load.
36. **Visual edit history** — compare image versions and restore any prior result.
37. **Region comments** — attach requests and reviewer notes to exact image areas.
38. **Brand-safe presets** — enforce approved colors, logos, spacing, and exclusions.
39. **Rights metadata panel** — record prompt, model, edits, license notes, and provenance.
40. **Multi-output art direction** — compare controlled variations without losing the brief.

## Teams, developers, and operations

41. **Permissioned shared memory** — share selected facts without exposing private history.
42. **Team review queues** — route research, images, and decisions to the right approver.
43. **Prompt-to-workflow templates** — turn successful work into permissioned team recipes.
44. **Scoped API keys** — restrict each key by endpoint, project, budget, IP, and expiry.
45. **API request explorer** — generate, run, and inspect safe sample calls in the docs.
46. **Spend guardrails** — warn, throttle, or stop at per-user and per-project budgets.
47. **Webhook delivery log** — inspect retries, signatures, payload versions, and failures.
48. **Idempotency dashboard** — detect and prevent duplicate charged operations.
49. **Provider fallback telemetry** — expose latency, failure, and fallback without brand leakage.
50. **Abuse and incident console** — review hashed signals, appeals, limits, and response steps.

## Recommended release order

- **Launch safety:** scoped keys, spend guardrails, request tracing, abuse console, and idempotency.
- **Vurenn differentiation:** claim ledger, contradiction radar, memory receipts, and truth report.
- **Project depth:** living briefs, file graph, formula audit, and permissioned shared memory.
- **Media quality:** interruption-aware voice, edit history, region comments, and rights metadata.
